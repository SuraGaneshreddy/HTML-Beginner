Upcoming Events

1. International Cat Day Celebration

Every year on August 8th, cat lovers around the world come together to celebrate International Cat Day — and this year’s event promises to be bigger and better than ever! From interactive cat toy workshops to feline wellness talks by leading vets, pet cafes and animal shelters across major cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore will host special programs and adoption drives.

This event isn’t just a celebration — it’s a chance to raise awareness about responsible pet ownership and to support local rescue centers. If you’re a cat parent, this is the perfect time to pamper your fur baby, and if you’re looking to adopt, many shelters will have waived adoption fees on the day. Keep an eye on local listings or visit your nearest pet center to take part!

2. Pet Fed India 2025 – Coming to Major Cities This Winter

Pet Fed India, often called "India’s biggest pet festival," is returning in late 2025 with events planned in Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore. With activities like off-leash play zones, fashion shows for pets, agility games, and even pet-friendly shopping, it’s the ultimate day out for pets and pet parents alike.

The festival also features adoption booths, expert talks, grooming zones, and plenty of food stalls for humans and pets. Whether you’re bringing Mr. Whiskers along or just want to meet hundreds of adorable animals, Pet Fed is a must-visit. Early bird tickets usually go live by September, so mark your calendars!

Past Events

1. WCF International Cat Show – February 1–2, 2025 (Hyderabad)

Held at the HITEX Exhibition Center in Hyderabad, this prestigious cat show was nationally and internationally judged. Organized by the Indian Cat Club and aligned with the World Cat Federation, it showcased various breeds, attracted thousands of visitors, and featured competitions and vendor stalls.

2. Kolkata Pet Expo 2025 – June 14–15, 2025 (Kolkata)

The Kolkata Pet Expo took place on June 14–15, 2025, at Science City. It drew families and pet lovers to enjoy exhibitions, pet workshops, interactive booths, adoption initiatives, and more.
