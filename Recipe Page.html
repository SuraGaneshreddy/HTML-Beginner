Preparing Biryani

Here is a simple biryani recipe that you
can follow at home.This version is flavorful,
moderately spicy,and easy enough for begginers.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmathi rice(soaked for 30 minutes)
  • water(for boiling)
  • 1-2 bay leaves
  • 4 cloves
  • 2-3 cgreen cardamons
  • 1 small piece cinnamon
  • Salt(to taste)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil and ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot or handi.
  2. Add sliced onions and fry until golden brown.
  3. Add marinated chicken and cook on medium
    flame for 10-15 minutes till chicken is nearly
    done and oil starts separating.

    4. 4.Layering Biryani:

    1. Lower the flame.
    2. Spread half-cooked rice over the chicken.
    3. Sprinkle chopped mint,coriander, saffron milk(optional), fried onions.
    4. Repeat another layer if needed.

    5.Dum(Steam cooking):

    1. Cover the pot with a tight lid (or seal with dough).
    2. Cook on very low flame for 20 minutes (use a tawa under the pot to avoid burning)
    3. let it rest for 10 minutes after switching off the flame.
